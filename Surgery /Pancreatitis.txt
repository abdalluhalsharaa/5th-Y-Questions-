Question 12:
Which vasopressor is the first-line agent for the management of septic shock 
unresponsive to fluid resuscitation?
A) Dopamine
B) Epinephrine
C) Norepinephrine
D) Dobutamine
E) Phenylephrine
Correct Answer: C) Norepinephrine
Explanation: Surviving Sepsis Guidelines recommend Norepinephrine as the first-line vasopressor 
for septic shock to maintain MAP >65 mmHg.

Question 14:
The external branch of the superior laryngeal nerve provides motor innervation 
to which muscle?
A) Thyroarytenoid
B) Lateral cricoarytenoid
C) Posterior cricoarytenoid
D) Cricothyroid
E) Interarytenoid
Correct Answer: D) Cricothyroid
Explanation: The external branch of the superior laryngeal nerve innervates the cricothyroid 
muscle. All other intrinsic laryngeal muscles are innervated by the recurrent laryngeal nerve.
