Question 1:
A 35-year-old female presents with palpitations and is found to be in atrial 
fibrillation. Her physical examination is unremarkable, and she has no history of 
hypertension, diabetes, or vascular disease. What is her CHA₂DS₂-VASc score?
A) 0
B) 1
C) 2
D) 3
E) 4
Correct Answer: B) 1
Explanation: The CHA₂DS₂-VASc score calculates stroke risk in atrial fibrillation. This patient 
receives 1 point for female sex. Being under 65 with no other comorbidities (CHF, HTN, Diabetes, 
Stroke, Vascular disease), she accumulates no further points.

Question 2:
Which of the following clinical findings is NOT typically associated with severe 
anemia?
A) Diastolic murmur
B) Systolic flow murmur
C) Tachycardia
D) Bounding pulses
E) Elevated cardiac output
Correct Answer: A) Diastolic murmur
Explanation: Severe anemia causes a hyperdynamic circulatory state (high output), leading to 
bounding pulses and systolic flow murmurs. Diastolic murmurs usually indicate structural valvular 
pathology (e.g., aortic regurgitation) rather than flow issues.
