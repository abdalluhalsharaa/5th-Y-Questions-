Question 51:
What is the single most important prognostic factor for localized cutaneous 
melanoma?
A) Tumor location
B) Breslow thickness (depth of invasion)
C) Clark level
D) Presence of ulceration
E) Patient age
Correct Answer: B) Breslow thickness (depth of invasion)
Explanation: The vertical thickness of the tumor (Breslow depth) is the strongest predictor of 
survival and metastasis in localized melanoma and guides surgical margins and lymph node 
staging.

Question 87:
What is the most common hereditary hypercoagulable state (thrombophilia)?
A) Protein C deficiency
B) Protein S deficiency
C) Antithrombin III deficiency
D) Factor V Leiden mutation
E) Prothrombin gene mutation
Correct Answer: D) Factor V Leiden mutation
Explanation: Factor V Leiden (resistance to activated Protein C) is the most common genetic cause 
of venous thrombosis.
