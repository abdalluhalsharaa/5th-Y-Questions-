A physician who has just mastered patient-centered communication skills conducts consultations. According to the lecture's data, approximately how long does each consultation take?
A) 7.8 minutes
B) 8.5 minutes
C) 11 minutes
D) 13 minutes
Correct Answer: B) 8.5 minutes

According to a survey published in the Archives of Internal Medicine, what proportion of doctors do not always inform patients of abnormal test results, particularly when mildly abnormal?
A) 1 in 5
B) 1 in 4
C) 1 in 3
D) 1 in 2
Correct Answer: A) 1 in 5
